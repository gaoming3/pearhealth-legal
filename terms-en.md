## 1. Who we are

Pear Health is an iOS app that helps you organize and understand your lab reports, operated by Dolores Education Inc. ("we"). You can upload a PDF or a photo of a lab report, and the app recognizes the results (analyte name, value, unit, reference range, test date), helps you organize them, track trends, and plan retests and reminders. You can also share selected content with family members or caregivers through an invite link (the "care circle"), read health articles and research summaries, and view supplement product information.
