# Pear Health Help

[中文](../help/)

## How do I upload a lab report?

In the app, choose upload. You can upload a PDF or a photo of a lab report. The original file is stored in private cloud storage.

## Where does recognition run, and why do I need to review it?

Recognition runs by default **on your phone**, using the text recognition built into iOS. This step does not use any cloud recognition service (the original file is still stored in private cloud storage, as described above). Recognition can make mistakes, so results are shown to you item by item for review and confirmation before they are saved. Always refer to the original lab report.

## What if a unit is not recognized?

Unrecognized units are kept as the original text for you to confirm or correct during review.

## How do I invite family members? How do I revoke access?

In the Care Circle, generate an invite link and send it to a family member or caregiver. The link is valid for 72 hours. Invited people only see what you choose to share. You can revoke access at any time.

## How do I export my data?

You can request an export of all your data inside the app. You will receive a ZIP file, and the download link is valid for 15 minutes.

## How do I delete my account? Can I cancel?

You can request account deletion inside the app. There is a 7-day cooling-off period during which you can cancel the request. After that, the account and its data are deleted.

## How do I turn off Apple Health access?

Turn it off anytime in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices. Apple Health data is only used on your phone for the "Today" view and is not uploaded to our servers.
