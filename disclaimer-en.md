# Pear Health Disclaimer

[中文](../disclaimer/)

Pear Health helps you organize and understand your lab reports, and provides general health information and lifestyle suggestions.

Pear Health is **not a medical device**. It does not diagnose, it does not prescribe, and it is not a substitute for a doctor.

- Information in the app is for reference only.
- Recognition results may contain errors; always refer to the original lab report.
- Reference ranges vary between testing institutions.
- For any changes to medication or treatment, consult your doctor; rely on the original report and your doctor's advice.
- In an emergency, call your local emergency number immediately.

Effective date: November 3, 2026
